About Me

Who I Am

I'm Ferdous Foysal Hridoy, a Computer Science & Engineering student at Daffodil International University with a deep passion for software engineering and the digital craft. I believe great software is built at the intersection of creativity and technical rigor.

My curiosity naturally gravitates toward WebRTC and real-time communication systems, AI-powered web experiences, and modern full-stack architecture. I enjoy exploring how emerging technologies can be applied to build tools that genuinely matter.

I approach learning the same way I approach code with deliberate curiosity, breaking problems apart until they're understood, then assembling elegant solutions.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me experimenting with new frameworks, contributing to open source, or exploring how AI can reshape the way we build on the web.
